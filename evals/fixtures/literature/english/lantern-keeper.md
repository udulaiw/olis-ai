# The Lantern Keeper

## The text

The keeper climbs the winding stair,
the sea below him grey and wide;
the lantern wakes, the only flare
that keeps the boats from the hungry tide.

## Notes

The keeper is alone with his duty: the lantern is personified as it "wakes", and the tide is called hungry.
