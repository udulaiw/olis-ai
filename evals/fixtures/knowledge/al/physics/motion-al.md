# Kinematics

## Speed and velocity

Speed is the magnitude of velocity. Velocity is the rate of change of displacement, v = ds/dt, so instantaneous speed needs differentiation of the displacement function.

## SUVAT

For constant acceleration use v = u + at, s = ut + 1/2 at^2.
