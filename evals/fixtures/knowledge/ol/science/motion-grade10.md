# Motion

## Speed

Speed is the distance travelled per unit time. At O/L we use speed = distance / time and the units m/s and km/h. Average speed is total distance divided by total time.

## Distance-time graphs

On a distance-time graph the gradient is the speed. A horizontal line means the object is at rest.
